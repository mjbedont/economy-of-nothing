🎧 Audio Podcast

A chip smuggling headline, a pun about Wongs, and a Singapore shipping loophole everyone has known about for years send the Captain and Mr. Green hunting for the real story, which is that the headline is never the story. Mr. Green calls the AI trade the perfect fugazi, because you can call a cotton candy machine AI and nobody will question it, while the Captain works out how a bailout gets sold once there is a flag to wrap it in. Grandpa knows about NVIDIA now, which means it is time to sell.

Then the culture gets bought. GTA VI is about to take over every screen, Live Nation survived its monopoly trial by owning every venue, and Dane Cook may be to blame. Mr. Green thinks people will go into debt for experiences and that the metaverse might finally arrive as a video game, the Captain thinks a generational opportunity is just a generational opportunity to take your money, and a sandwich costs forty cents more than it did yesterday. Everyone is escaping into the Matrix and tipping the driver, and the off switch is the only leverage anyone has left, whether that is a Sunday outage or a Facebook that goes dark until Congress behaves.

Finally, the mailbag arms the Moonshine. Listeners offer a bat for hitting mailboxes at Skull Pirates, grappling arms from Outlaw Star, and a laser that is still firing in season ten. The Darth Talon guy abandons kidnapping George Lucas for hiding in his sister's trunk with snacks, and the Captain raises the bathroom question. Ryan gets addressed, and the show gets reminded it is a living show. It all ends in digital feudalism: pick a lord, Musk, Altman, or Amodei, because you have to be on somebody's set of nuts. Mr. Green would like to go back to the Motorola Razr, and the phone always wins.
