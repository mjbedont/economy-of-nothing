🎧 Audio Podcast

The crew opens by punching up Alan Michaels, a nemesis who needs an eyepatch, a cut over the eye, and a much better jacket, right before some ill-advised magic turns somebody into an orc. From there it's a tavern on Draconaria, a shortage of canonical money, and a Scotch Captain nobody asked for.

The mailbag arrives on glitter scrolls: an AI expert with a nuclear engineering PhD predicts humans get replaced within a century, a listener in Australia wants the name of that philosophy book, and another has a plan to kidnap George Lucas over Darth Talon. A resident average guy pitch and a Kevin Hart credit card ad round out the letters.

Then it's Mitch McConnell, dead internet theory, ads nobody clicks, and a planet where the money is made up. Somewhere in there Mr. Green gets his dragon: a lime green Dino with old man scruff, dog eyes, broken little wings, and a taste for raw whole chickens. Alan Michaelson is in the tavern too, and he is not helpful.
