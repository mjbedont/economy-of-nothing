Mr. Green's feet hurt, and the Captain would like everyone to know that this is the most time anything has ever wasted of his. Plantar fasciitis becomes a seminar on legacy, third-party viewing of yourself, and why every statue fight is between people who do not know who the statue is. Remember the Titans gets its graveyard speech reviewed as motivational strategy, The Long Walk gets a three-warnings business model, and a 2D World War I platformer teaches the Captain what good art feels like. AI, as a result, is bullshit.

Then the Dark Orbit gets a name. The Captain asks the algorithm about Sodom and Gomorrah, gets handed one specific Bible, and reads Genesis 19 to find out why the wording sounds nothing like the Bible. Mr. Green reads it aloud. Lot is bad at everything. The Mandela Effect gets a cornucopia, religion gets a rich-person defense plan, and the internet keeps choosing the same scripture every time you ask. Known unknowns, unknown unknowns, and why aliens will not land on a planet of pigs at a trough.

Along the way: Buc-ee's, Dolly Parton's gas station rival, GLP-1s as a replacement for healthcare, and the Captain declining to be a software bro. Then back to Planet Fun, where the mystery box finds out it has nowhere to go, Skulk gets asked for his number, and Scoliosis borrows a time rewind device from Deadpool to fix the whole thing. Next week, with passion.
