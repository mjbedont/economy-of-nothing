This week: a sandwich order that outranks a hot extraction, a mustache nobody realizes is the key, and an economy that's been edging everyone toward a cliff for years.

Skulk hauls the deactivated Marshal back to the boss and, in a moment of tragic efficiency, leaves the mustache behind — unaware it's the only thing standing between Nothing Corp and the mystery box. While the Captain lines up his shot, Mr. Green orders a chicken Philly with extra chipotle mayo and somehow, from a gas station parking lot, arrives at the entire architecture of the modern economy: AI, crypto, SpaceX, and why nobody under 40 has gotten a real raise in a decade.

Plus: a Teletubbies interrogation nobody asked for, a running Nolan-bashing segment that may or may not survive the season, and a mailbag question so devastating that Mr. Green demands to know who sent it. He does not find out. Neither do we.
