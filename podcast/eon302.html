The Captain is a wizard from the future, Mr. Green is disgruntled, and AI is going to kill us all — but not like they're saying. This week's Patreon exclusive opens with a live-puppet rig turning Mike's own face into real-time Captain animation, which the Captain immediately puts to use introducing himself to Mr. Green as a wizard from the future.

From there it's a full teardown of FastPass economics — slotting fees, category managers, and how paying to be special just means paying to screw over the person behind you — into an AI doom-mongering spiral covering the "Pause Giant AI Experiments" open letter, Skynet comparisons, and a prediction that stocks, Bitcoin, and gold are all about to take turns crashing into each other.

Plus: a rant on subscription-creep car features, a defense of Toyota for not doing it, and a closing argument that the freedom of speech got knocked down within a couple years of the Constitution and nobody's shocked. Mr. Green stages a coup, demands a raise in "imaginary math," and the crew ships off to Drakonaria to shake down a robot for a new mission.
