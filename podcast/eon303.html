Before a single frame gets shot, the crew storyboards the whole Dracon Area landing out loud — and quickly talks themselves out of ever actually meeting the queen this week. Too much episode, they decide, so instead of a throne room it's a truck, a landing sequence, and a walk to the front gate.

From there it's an icebreaker that spirals into a full benchmarking system for what makes a day good or bad, a bearish stock market theory built entirely off vibes and trading against their own trajectory, and a sincere little monologue about why earnestness — not integrity, not values, just earnestness — is the whole show's secret weapon.

They end up halfway to the castle at a tavern instead, working through the two-to-three beer zone, a Kura Revolving Sushi tangent about charm bracelets and mediocre drink robots, and a pitch to put actual ads on the truck now that they're "in the metaverse, baby." Naturally, it closes with Mr. Green becoming a father and nobody explaining anything further.
