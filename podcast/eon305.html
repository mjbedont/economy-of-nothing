🎧 Audio Podcast

Last week's episode tanked, so the crew blames the algorithm, plugs Sponsor Needed, and decides the problem is a villain nobody clicked on. Two pitches hit the table: a bar fight, or a Rick and Morty style ballad that ends with the Captain shooting him. Alan Michaels gets a backstory either way: a mercenary who wouldn't pull the trigger, a system that betrayed him while he was trying to betray the system, and a picture of Mr. Green on his prison wall.

Then comes the voice workshop. Jason Statham is tried and rejected, Stallone is attempted, and Alan lands as a Tired Ben Affleck Batman on the way to the donut shop. Everything bad has already happened to him. He is dispassionately depressed. At the bar he gets one line before the Captain shoots him in the head, declares it non-canon, and the crew grabs the egg and finishes the Draconaria delivery.

Afterward it's a production reality check on what can actually be animated, a rant about Marvel and Lantern budgets, and a long defense of Arrow. The episode closes with Mr. Green waking up in a Nothing Corporation hospital bed, 37 monies in debt, with a classic adventure promised next week.
