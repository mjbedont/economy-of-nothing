🎧 Audio Podcast

Still stranded on Planet Fun, the Captain and Mr. Green open with a question about literacy and end up auditing the whole economy. Unreliable narrators, a safe space that is not safe, and the news as a comfort loop that tells you exactly what you already believe. Somewhere in there, Neil deGrasse Tyson gets a trial and Bill Nye gets a character reference.

Then the books close. The crew started with 15 credits to get in, paid $2 a head at the gate, and bought three bathroom tokens nobody has used. The Captain wonders if bathroom tokens are a store of value. Mr. Green has a quarter-a-visit story from the Autobahn, which turns out to be a VR headset, which raises a few questions about who has dementia. From there the loop appears: minutes became data, data became products, and now Xbox wants to bill you by the minute for the games you already bought. Products into time, time into data, data into products, until the console just takes blood.

Along the way: the middle class is a rumor, everyone is a status quo warrior, America loves a good punch down, and agentic shopping is for people who hate shopping. Mr. Green learns who actually wrote the amendments, and it was not Hamilton. The Captain promises to watch all four hours of the musical the day Patreon hits $1,000 a month. Until then, hope in one hand, you know the rest.
