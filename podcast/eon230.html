This week: the mystery box is finally back in the Captain's hands, and getting it off the ship is the whole problem.

The Captain makes a run for the hangar bay while the Skull Pirates open fire, Green rolls out a brand-new monetary maneuver — this one's for family — and nobody's totally sure what actually happened to Marshal Roberts. Meanwhile a low-level grunt named 105C learns the fastest way to survive a bad boss is to have someone else take the fall, and Bonehead very nearly lets fifty pizzas onto a pirate warship.

Plus: the Greek chorus theory of the Skulls, a personal pitch to Alan Ritchson, an argument about whether anything is actually new anymore, and a mailbag letter that turns into a real conversation about meaning.
