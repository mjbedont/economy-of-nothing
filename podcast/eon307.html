Last week's recording was cut short by a business retreat, so Mike and Mike come back with the episode that wasn't an episode, a dying wireless mic, and a firm belief that the perfect episode needs Skulk and Scoliosis. Before the show gets going: a Tom Cruise vanity project, a Sorkin sequel that opened to almost nobody, and the Jackass movie everyone remembered as better than it was.

Then the workshop. The Captain and Mr. Green have a delivery and no destination, so Mike pitches a Rollercoaster Tycoon planet where the park has no exit and the only currency is your time. Mr. Green cannot resist one ride. One ride becomes a cotton candy, a cotton candy becomes an Icee, and the Captain refuses to put the box down for any of them. Skulk, now a mix of John Lennon and Yogi Bear, explains the plan to a boss who is taking full credit for it: keep Mr. Green riding until he throws up, then grab the mystery box. Neither of them can explain the data center bolted to the side of the ship or what it does to the utility bill.

On the ground, the D1CK robots run the Family Fun Time entrance, and nobody has the credits for the fast pass. The Captain has 37 credits and a fee schedule that wants 39. The bathroom tokens are negotiated. "Stupid" turns out to be a foul word. By strike two, the security bots are on their way, and the episode ends with a run. Stick around for the post-show: the steering wheel on the Moonshine does nothing, and Mr. Green's driving finally gets explained.
